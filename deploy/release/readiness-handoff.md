# Credential, store, cohort and operations handoff

This metadata-only template records external evidence for controlled production
account custody, Play/App Store custody, a witnessed signing ceremony, UAT
consent/training/completion, circle and host readiness, and support and
trust-and-safety coverage.

Only opaque evidence references and role references belong here. Never include
secrets, signing material, participant or staff identities, contact details,
free-form feedback, schedules, store payloads or credentials. Issuer and
reviewer must be distinct. Missing, pending, synthetic, stale, substituted or
replayed evidence fails closed.

Validation does not provision credentials, submit an application, recruit or
contact a cohort, schedule staff, change coverage, or approve production.
