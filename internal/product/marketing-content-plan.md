# Obiara public marketing content plan

## Purpose

The public site introduces Obiara without exposing the signed-in product shell.
It should feel like an invitation into a trusted Ghanaian social space, not a
catalogue of features or a conventional dating landing page.

`apps/marketing` owns public discovery, campaign pages, search metadata and
brand storytelling. `apps/web` remains the authenticated member experience.

## Audience and page job

Primary audience: Ghanaian adults who want deliberate connection and are wary
of high-volume dating products.

Secondary audience: circle hosts, trusted introducers and early community
partners.

The homepage must communicate three ideas quickly:

1. Obiara begins with voice and character.
2. Trust and community shape access.
3. Hosted circles create a more human way to meet.

## Homepage story and media

| Sequence | Story | Media requirement | Primary action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Meet properly | Wide Accra courtyard portrait with space for the main message | Explore the compound |
| Voice | Be heard before being judged | Intimate vertical portrait recording a voice introduction | Continue scrolling |
| Compound | A compound, not a feed | Typographic map of Fie, Circles and Rooms | Understand the model |
| Trust | Access is earned and boundaries are visible | Deliberately quiet editorial section | Read the principles |
| Fires | Good meetings can be hosted | Wide dusk image of a hosted courtyard circle | Enter Obiara |
| Close | Come as a whole person | Large closing statement | Enter the member app |

## Content rules

- Keep paragraphs below 35 words and let imagery carry atmosphere.
- Do not publish fabricated member counts, testimonials or safety outcomes.
- Do not imply nationwide availability while the first cohort is Accra-based.
- Show contemporary Ghana without tourism tropes or generic stock imagery.
- Use Outfit across public, member web, admin and mobile.
- Every new campaign must have a named audience, one conversion goal, real
  media rights and an expiry or review date.

## Media backlog

- Commission or license real member and host photography before public launch.
- Capture short ambient courtyard and city clips for a later motion-led hero.
- Record three consented voice introductions for an interactive audio story.
- Produce a host portrait series for circle and partner recruitment.
- Create social crops from approved master assets rather than stretching page
  imagery into every channel.

Generated imagery in the initial implementation is art direction for the first
build. Replace it with consented production photography before launch.

## Measurement

Track only meaningful public intent: hero-to-story progression, member-entry
clicks and campaign source. Do not add attention traps or invasive session
replay by default. Analytics implementation requires a separate privacy review.
