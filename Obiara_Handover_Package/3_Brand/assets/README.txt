PALETTE (Sunset Marigold)
  Marigold      #FF9F1C   the gathered figures, actions, the drum
  Hibiscus      #FF4D6D   the newcomer, love accents, emotional emphasis
  Deep Plum     #3A0E2E   dark ground / Night Fie / primary text on light
  Blush Cream   #FFF3E6   light surfaces / text on dark / the hearth on dark
  Palm Green    #12A67C   growth & success states

FOLDERS
  logo/svg     Editable vector masters (marks, lockups, wordmarks, app tiles)
  logo/png     Production PNGs ~2000px: _transparent + light/dark/marigold grounds
  icons        App icons, favicons (512/192/48), profile avatars (1024, circle-crop safe)
  social       Facebook cover, LinkedIn banner, X header, IG flyer 1080x1080, Story flyer 1080x1920
  mockups      T-shirt treatments, billboard grammar, business cards
  fonts        Fraunces + Plus Jakarta Sans (static + variable) under SIL OFL (licenses included)

NAMING
  asset-form-variant_background.png   (suffixes _on-shea/_on-umber/_on-gold denote
  light / dark-plum / marigold grounds respectively). "_transparent" = alpha channel.

RULES IN ONE BREATH
  Marigold figures, one hibiscus newcomer, shared hearth. Clearspace = 1/4 mark height.
  Min sizes: mark 24px/8mm, lockup 96px/28mm. Never restyle, rotate, stretch, recolour.
  Marigold/hibiscus never as body text on light. No URLs/handles in artwork until secured.
  When in doubt: would the aunties approve?
